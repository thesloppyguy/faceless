## Intro

Not all drinks with the same alcohol look the same.
That's the surprise in a huge UK drinking study.

## Body

Researchers followed over three hundred thousand UK adults for a decade.
Heavy drinking raised death risk no matter the beverage.
High intake meant about twenty-four percent more deaths overall.
At low and moderate levels, the type of drink split.
Beer, cider, and spirits lined up with higher mortality.
Similar amounts of wine lined up with lower risk.
Moderate wine drinkers had twenty-one percent lower heart-death risk.
Even low beer or spirits tied to nine percent more heart deaths.
Catch: this is observational, not proof wine protects you.
Wine often comes with meals and healthier habits.
Beer and spirits more often come with messier ones.
So this isn't a prescription to start drinking wine.

## Outro

Watch for trials that assign the drink, not just the diary.
Until then, less is still safer than swapping brands.
