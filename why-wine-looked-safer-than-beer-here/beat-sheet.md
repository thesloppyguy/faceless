# Beat sheet — Why Wine Looked Safer Than Beer Here

**Script:** why-wine-looked-safer-than-beer-here/script.md
**Treatment:** stop motion · posterize judder on all motion · vintage film
**Beats:** 16

## Continuity

**World:** One scratched dark-wood pub table under an underexposed plaster wall; papers and glasses swap on the same crop. No new rooms unless the line forces a meal plate or a diary stack.
**Background plate:** `BG-pub-wall` — dingy plaster, one crooked picture frame, analog dust in the corners, held underexposure.
**Filter stack:** `FILTER-vintage-hold` — grain, vignette, edge blur, analog dust, slight underexposure
**Hero objects:** `PROP-three-glasses` — wine tulip, pint, short spirit glass, same fill height unless a beat overfills them; `PROP-study-printout` — UK cohort headline sheet with decade span; `PROP-drink-diaries` — stacked paper drinking logs; `PROP-heart-glyph` — simple anatomical heart stamp, not a cartoon.

## Intro

### intro-1 · same alcohol, different look

**The Voice lines**

Not all drinks with the same alcohol look the same.

**What it becomes on screen**

Hold a medium still of `PROP-three-glasses` in a straight row on the table: wine tulip, pint, short spirit glass, each filled to the same measured line. A small paper tag on the table edge reads the same alcohol units on all three. Crop out faces and the rest of the room; only wall, table lip, and glasses. Vintage hold: dusty, slightly underexposed, labels readable. Outgoing still: the three equal fills, tags attached.

**Transition in**

Cold-open hold. No prior still. Background, table, glasses, and filter land together in one frame.

**Assets needed**

- **Midground:** `SET-scratched-table` — dark wood, rings, crumbs
- **Foreground:** `PROP-three-glasses` — equal fill height
- **Characters:** none
