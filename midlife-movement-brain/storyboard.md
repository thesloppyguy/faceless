# Storyboard — A little movement in midlife could pay off for your brain years later

**Script:** midlife-movement-brain/script.md
**Treatment:** stop motion · posterize judder on all motion · vintage film
**Beats:** 14

## Continuity

**World:** Midlife health desk and clinic collage: paper charts, simple icons, no faces. San Antonio as a small city silhouette, not a travelogue.
**Background plate:** `BG-DESK` — warm taupe paper desk with soft vignette corners; holds for the whole reel unless a beat swaps to `BG-CITY` (flat San Antonio skyline strip).
**Filter stack:** `FILTER-CLINIC` — film grain, soft vignette, slight edge blur, sparse dust, mild underexposure.
**Hero objects:**
- `PROP-HEART` — simple red heart icon (cardboard cutout feel)
- `PROP-BRAIN` — simple brain icon (same cutout language)
- `PROP-WALK` — walking figure silhouette or sneakers mid-step
- `PROP-GLUCOSE` — blood-drop / meter reading card showing `126`
- `PROP-COHORT` — two stacked people-count cards (`402`, `HALF / HALF`)

## Intro

### intro-1 · heart now, brain later

**The Voice lines**

What you do for your heart in midlife may shape your brain later.

**What it becomes on screen**

Centered on `BG-DESK`, `PROP-HEART` lands hard left of frame. A dashed arrow steps right in three clicks toward `PROP-BRAIN`, which pops in smaller and slightly faded. Type cards do not appear yet. The still ends with heart bright, arrow mid-span, brain waiting.

**On-screen words**

none

**Emotional beat**

Quiet hook: cause now, effect later.

**Signature animation**

Stepped arrow: three discrete positions from heart toward brain.

**Transition in**

Hold open on empty desk for one beat, then layer-land heart.

**Assets needed**

- **Background:** `BG-DESK`
- **Midground:** dashed path / arrow segments
- **Foreground:** `PROP-HEART`, `PROP-BRAIN`
- **Characters:** none
- **Props:** none (icons only)
- **Filter:** `FILTER-CLINIC`

### intro-2 · decades later

**The Voice lines**

Not next year. Decades later, when thinking starts to fade.

**What it becomes on screen**

Same desk. A calendar strip jumps: `NOW` → `+10` → `+20` → `+30` in posterize steps. Brain opacity drops one notch on the last jump; a faint fog or soft grey overlay sits over the brain only. Heart stays sharp. Ends on faded brain + `+30` card.

**On-screen words**

`NOW` · `+30`

**Emotional beat**

Time stretch; mild unease without scare tactics.

**Signature animation**

Calendar cards jump-replace; brain dims one step on the final card.

**Transition in**

Hold heart/brain; snap-cut calendar strip into midground.

**Assets needed**

- **Background:** `BG-DESK`
- **Midground:** calendar strip cards
- **Foreground:** `PROP-HEART`, `PROP-BRAIN` (dimmed)
- **Characters:** none
- **Props:** type cards `NOW`, `+30`
- **Filter:** `FILTER-CLINIC`
