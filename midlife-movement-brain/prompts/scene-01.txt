# Scene 01 — Heart now, brain later

**Result:** Warm taupe paper desk fills the vertical frame. A cardboard red heart cutout lands hard left. A dashed arrow advances toward the right in three discrete steps. A smaller, slightly faded brain cutout pops in on the right. No captions. Stop-motion / posterize judder on every move. Vintage clinic grade.

**Duration:** ~4.0s (120 frames @ 30fps)  
**VO:** What you do for your heart in midlife may shape your brain later.  
**Storyboard beat:** intro-1  
**Starts on:** empty `BG-DESK` (film open)  
**Ends on:** heart left bright, dashed arrow mid-span after 3 steps, brain right smaller/faded. Scene 02 must open on this exact still.

## Actions (in order)

1. Hold empty desk for a short beat.
2. Layer-land `PROP-HEART` left with one judder settle.
3. Step dashed arrow segment 1, then 2, then 3 (posterize, no ease glide).
4. Pop `PROP-BRAIN` right, smaller and lower opacity than the heart.
5. Hold end still through VO tail.
